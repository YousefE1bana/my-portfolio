## Fonts, icons and services

- Caveat is self-hosted. Its SIL Open Font License is retained at [Caveat-OFL.txt](../src/assets/Caveat-OFL.txt).
- Space Grotesk, IBM Plex Sans and JetBrains Mono are served through Google Fonts with `display=swap`. Upstream font licenses apply: [Space Grotesk](https://github.com/floriankarsten/space-grotesk), [IBM Plex](https://github.com/IBM/plex), [JetBrains Mono](https://github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono).
- Interface icons use [Lucide](https://lucide.dev/license), distributed under the ISC license. Social brand identifiers are used to label their destinations.
- The optional music player is Spotify's official iframe, loaded only after the visitor opens it. Playlist playback and third-party availability are controlled by Spotify.
